# AutoFace v0.35.9.5 — Atlas Reflection

- Renames the user-facing feature from `Atlas AI reflection` to `Atlas Reflection`.
- Removes the technical Gemini checkbox, `AVAILABLE/OFF` badge and `GEMINI EXPLANATION` label.
- Makes Atlas Reflection visible consistently; when the AI service is unavailable, users see a normal product message rather than environment-variable instructions.
- First use now presents a clear consent prompt explaining what relationship data is sent for the reflection.
- Remembers Atlas Reflection consent on the user's relationship profile after approval.
- Uses structured JSON generation for profile reflections to prevent malformed fragments such as stray colons, bullets or truncated answer labels.
- Reflection output is sanitised into:
  - a natural relationship-style summary,
  - likely strengths,
  - neutral themes worth exploring.
- AI Reflection remains explanatory only and cannot change deterministic compatibility or authenticity.
