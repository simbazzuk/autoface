# AutoFace v0.2 Security & Identity Model

## Scope
v0.2 establishes secure account identity and a deterministic Authenticity Score. It does not collect matrimonial profile data or identity documents.

## Authenticity weights
- Email verified: 10
- Mobile verified: 15
- MFA/passkey: 10
- Identity verified: 30 (v0.3)
- Liveness verified: 20 (v0.3)
- Profile photo verified: 15 (v0.3)

## Zero-ID Storage
The application schema contains no fields or upload flows for passports, driving licences, raw selfies, biometric templates or identity-document numbers.

## Firebase setup
Enable Email/Password and Phone providers in Firebase Authentication. Add your local/deployed hostname under Authentication > Settings > Authorized domains. Phone verification uses Firebase reCAPTCHA.
