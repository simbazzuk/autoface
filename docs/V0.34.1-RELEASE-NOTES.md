# AutoFace v0.34.1 — Structured Atlas Relationship Choices

Replaces the three large free-text relationship questions with selectable structured tiles.

## Atlas relationship experience
- Weekend: choose up to 4 tiles.
- Long-term relationship priorities: choose up to 5.
- Relationship non-negotiables: choose up to 5.
- Keeps one smaller optional free-text field for context that the tiles do not capture.
- Existing relationship pace and compatibility scales remain unchanged.

## Data
Adds:
- `weekendPreferences`
- `relationshipPriorities`
- `nonNegotiablePreferences`
- `relationshipContext`

The legacy `idealWeekend`, `whatMattersMost`, and `nonNegotiables` strings remain populated from the selected tiles for backwards compatibility with Atlas AI prompts and existing server logic.

## Testing
Synthetic seed relationship profiles now include the structured selections.

Firestore rules are updated for the new fields and must be deployed.
