# AutoFace v0.34.3 — Engaging Empty Discovery

Turns an empty Discover page into an active, reassuring member experience.

## Empty Discovery
When a member is eligible but there is nobody currently worth presenting, AutoFace now explains that:
- their Profile is active
- Atlas is ready
- they are visible for introductions
- AutoFace does not show filler profiles simply to populate the page

The member can review preferences, open My Introductions or review previous profiles.

## While You Wait
Adds a lightweight `This or That` activity with 10 lifestyle questions.

Important boundaries:
- answers are stored only in the member's browser using localStorage
- answers are explicitly labelled `just for fun`
- answers do not change compatibility
- answers do not affect who Atlas recommends
- no new Firestore collection or rules are required

This gives members something engaging to do when the curated introduction pool is temporarily empty without weakening Atlas recommendation standards.
