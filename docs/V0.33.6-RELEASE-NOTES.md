# AutoFace v0.33.6 — Navigation Dropdown Fix

Fixes sticky desktop My AutoFace and Trust menus.

- Click the same menu again to close it.
- Click outside to close.
- Press Escape to close.
- Selecting a menu item closes the dropdown.
- Opening My AutoFace closes Trust and vice versa.
- Opening either also closes the account dropdown.

No routing, authentication, Discovery, Atlas or data logic changes.
