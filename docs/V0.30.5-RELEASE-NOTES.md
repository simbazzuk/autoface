# AutoFace v0.30.5 — Richer Profiles & Career Preferences

## Profile
Adds structured profession and education details:
- job title
- professional area
- employment type
- career importance
- education level
- subject / field
- institution

## Discovery cards
Cards now explain more about the person before the Atlas recommendation:
- About section
- profession and professional area
- education level / field
- Sikh practice, diet, appearance and caste/community when supplied
- career-preference fit badges

## Career & education preferences
Discovery Preferences adds:
- Doesn't matter
- Similar career outlook preferred
- Preferred professional areas
- Education preference

These are deliberately **soft preferences** in v0.30.5. They do not exclude a candidate and do not alter the official deterministic compatibility percentage. They provide context to the member and Atlas explanation layer.

No new Firestore collection is required; fields are added to existing `profiles` and `discoveryPreferences` documents.
