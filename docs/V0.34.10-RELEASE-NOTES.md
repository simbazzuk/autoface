# AutoFace v0.34.10 — Technology Meets Matrimony Positioning

## What changed

The homepage now makes the AutoFace technology proposition explicit with the new hero strapline:

> **Where technology meets matrimony**

The supporting hero paragraph now explains that AutoFace combines compatibility, shared values, Atlas AI and authenticity signals to create more considered Sikh introductions.

## Why

This distinguishes AutoFace from traditional matchmaking services without positioning technology as a replacement for human choice. The existing message around fewer profiles, explainable compatibility and mutual introductions remains intact.

## Scope

Homepage copy only. No Firebase, authentication, discovery, matching, recommendation or verification logic has been modified.
