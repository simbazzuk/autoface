# AutoFace v0.36.1.6 - Chat Entitlements

Commercial entitlement test patch.

## Free
- Mutual introductions can open chat.
- Each Free member can send 5 messages per introduction.
- The allowance is per sender, so both people get their own 5 messages.
- Remaining message count is shown clearly.
- After 5 sent messages, the composer locks and points to Founding Membership.
- Obvious phone numbers, email addresses, URLs and social handles are blocked for Free messaging.

## Founding / Plus
- Unlimited messaging.
- Contact-detail sharing allowed.
- Existing Atlas and membership entitlements remain unchanged.

## Safety
- Block, report and unmatch remain available regardless of membership.
- Limits are enforced server-side, not just hidden in the UI.
- No Stripe/payment integration is included yet; use the existing entitlement harness to switch Free/Founding for testing.
