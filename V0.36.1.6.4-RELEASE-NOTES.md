# AutoFace v0.36.1.6.4 - Blocked Members + Unblock

This patch completes and clarifies the member-facing unblock journey.

## Behaviour
- Blocked members remain manageable under Account & Notifications > Safety & Blocking.
- The list shows the blocked member, location when available, block date and whether the block came from a safety report.
- `Unblock` requires confirmation.
- Unblocking removes the safety block but does NOT automatically restore the old introduction or conversation.
- After unblocking, the two people may become eligible for the normal Discovery / mutual-interest journey again.
- Safety reports remain on record after unblocking.
- Legacy/non-canonical block records are also removed safely.
- Existing block enforcement remains server-side and overrides messaging membership entitlements.

## Safety decision
A Founding/Plus membership cannot bypass a block. Unblocking is a deliberate action by the member who created the block.
