# AutoFace v0.36.1.6.2 - Chat Test Introduction Eligibility Fix

Fixes the Priya <-> Maya synthetic mutual introduction not appearing on My Introductions.

## Root cause
The v0.36.1.6.1 test harness created both profiles and the mutual `matches` record correctly, but the synthetic identity records had `photoVerified: false`.

AutoFace's real Introductions journey calls the same safe profile projection used by Discovery. That projection requires an authenticity score of at least 50. Email verification contributes 20 and Face Verification contributes 55, so the synthetic users were only scoring 20 and were deliberately filtered out.

## Fix
- Development-only Priya and Maya test identities now use `photoVerified: true` and `faceVerified: true`.
- The synthetic identity records are explicitly labelled `developmentChatHarness`.
- Existing production authenticity thresholds and verification logic are unchanged.
- Re-running `Prepare two Free test users` refreshes both identity records and the Priya/Maya mutual introduction.

Expected result:
- Priya > My Introductions shows Maya.
- Maya > My Introductions shows Priya.
- Introductions count becomes 1.
- Chat can then be opened and the 5-message Free allowance tested.
