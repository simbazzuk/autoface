# AutoFace v0.35.9.3 — Blocked Profiles & Unblock Management

- Adds a user-facing Blocked Profiles section to Account & Notifications.
- Shows exactly which profiles the signed-in user has blocked, including first name, broad location (when available), block date and whether the block came from a report.
- Adds a secure server-side Unblock action.
- Blocking confirmation in chat now names the person being blocked and explains where they can later be unblocked.
- Unblocking restores the original mutual introduction/conversation only when:
  - the match was closed by this user's block,
  - the other member has not independently blocked the user, and
  - the original match still exists.
- Safety reports are never removed by unblocking.
- Every unblock writes a security event.
- Blocking remains server-side enforced across messaging, interests, discovery and profile-photo access.
