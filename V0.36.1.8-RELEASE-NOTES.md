# AutoFace v0.36.1.8 - Membership Centre

Adds a dedicated `/membership` Membership Centre.

## Free member
- Shows AutoFace Free as the current plan.
- Shows the live number of Founder places remaining.
- Shows Free access versus locked Founder/Plus capabilities.
- Provides `Check my Founder eligibility`.
- Displays the five Founder eligibility checks after the user asks for a check.

## Founding Member
- Shows permanent Founding Member number.
- Shows Lifetime Founder benefits.
- Shows unlimited messaging/contact sharing/full Atlas access as membership capabilities.
- Makes clear no subscription is required to retain Founder status.

## AutoFace Plus
- Supports the Plus plan in the UI now.
- Shows the paid membership capability set.
- Billing area is intentionally a placeholder until payment integration is enabled.

## Account integration
The installer attempts to add a Membership Centre card to `app/account/page.tsx`.
If the Account page structure cannot be safely patched, `/membership` still works directly and the installer reports that the shortcut was skipped.

No payment integration is added in this release.
