# AutoFace v0.36.1.8.5 - Founding Member Discovery

Makes the Founding Programme more visible on the landing page.

- Live Founder strip with remaining places.
- Signed-out visitors get `Create my profile`.
- Signed-in Free members get `Check eligibility`.
- Existing Founders do not see the strip.
- Signed-out visitors see a welcome modal after about 1.4 seconds.
- Dismissed modal is suppressed for 24 hours in that browser.
- Modal is hidden when Founder places are full.
- Founder count comes from `/api/founding-program`; it is not hard-coded.
- No Founder allocation, membership or payment logic changes.
